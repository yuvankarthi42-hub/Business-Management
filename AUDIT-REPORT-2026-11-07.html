Verdict. The product works end to end. Billing, payments, purchases, the supplier ledger, branches, GST on bills, QR orders, the kitchen, tables, cancellations and returns all give consistent numbers on all three database setups. Duplicate bills and parallel bills are handled safely. Shop and branch data stayed isolated in every test. There are no Critical security issues.

Fix before go-live:

  • Return refunds on bills that mix GST rates (wrong refund and GST credit-note amounts).
  • Kitchen and QR screens stop updating after 5 idle minutes.
  • Low-privilege staff can read all customers and bills.
  • The campaign permission gap.
  • The javascript: QR link.
  • Two small regressions from the shop-cache change.

Biggest scale and cost lever: a bill takes 27–45 database round trips, one after another. At about 10 ms each that is 0.3–0.5 s per bill, and it is also what sets the hosting bill.

9. Recommended fix order

  1. Now (small): R-1 and R-2 (shop-cache regressions); F-6 (campaign permission); F-7 (QR link); F-2 (kitchen idle refresh).
  2. Before go-live: F-1 (returns / GST credit notes); F-5 and F-12 (server permissions and branch filter); F-8 and F-9 (stock on cancel, KOT reductions); F-15 to F-20 (validation, 500s, idempotency).
  3. For cost and scale: F-3 (fewer round trips per bill, which also fixes F-4 and F-14); F-10 (cache feature flags); F-11 (daily jobs fan-out); F-13 (check function memory).
  4. Hardening: F-21 to F-28, F-33 to F-35, and the Info items.
  5. Before deploy (pending from earlier): deploy, /migrate, smoke test, /ip-check verification, set PASSWORD_PEPPER and STRATUS_PRIVATE_BUCKET, rotate the exposed secrets, and recreate existing shop databases (shop-cache schema).

10. Scope and limits of this audit

  • No project code, schema, config or dependency was changed. Temporary test files were created and deleted (none remain).
  • Everything ran locally: the demo app for the UI, and local test servers (SQLite, fake Turso over HTTP, single DB) for APIs, security and load. No real or deployed host was contacted.
  • Not tested: real Turso and Catalyst latency; Catalyst instance concurrency; the Stratus bucket's headers; real WhatsApp, web push and payment providers; the admin UI pages (password not opened); past-day reports after back-dated changes; long rate-limit soak or DoS; whether a javascript: link actually runs in each browser.
  • Load figures come from an M3 laptop while the full suite was running. p50 and averages are reliable; p95 is inflated.
